# What is a Client?

A **Client** is the self-hosted node through which a data holder joins the %%DEPLOYED_PRODUCT_NAME%% network. It hosts your data locally, makes it discoverable, and enforces your institution's access rules, so you take part in the network while keeping control over your data.

With a Client you can:

- create cohorts and manage their data
- control who may run federated analyses on your data
- keep your site discoverable and take part in federated analyses

Deploy a Client if your organization holds data that should stay under local control. If you only want to use data and tools already available in the network, start with [Platform usage](../platform-usage/welcome.md) instead.
